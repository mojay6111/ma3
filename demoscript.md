# **THE MA3 DEMO SCRIPT**
*May 28 | Africa's Talking Transportation Hackathon*

---

### OPENING (20 seconds)
> "Every day, 3 million Nairobi commuters ask the same question — *lini ma3 inakuja?* When is the matatu coming? Nobody knows. Not the commuter at the stage, not the SACCO manager, not even the driver. Ma3 changes that — using real-time telemetry, machine learning, and Africa's Talking APIs to bring intelligence to the matatu ecosystem."

---

### THE PROBLEM (20 seconds)
> "Matatu SACCOs operate blind. No fleet visibility. No demand forecasting. Drivers are unsupervised. Commuters wait with zero information. And everything runs on cash. Ma3 solves all of this — without requiring anyone to download an app."

---

### LIVE DEMO — ACT 1: THE COMMUTER (30 seconds)
*Pick up phone. Open SMS.*

> "Grace is at Westlands stage. She has a Safaricom line — no smartphone, no data bundle. She texts one word — **WESTLANDS** — to the Ma3 shortcode."

*Send the SMS.*

> "Instantly, Ma3's LSTM model predicts the arrival time based on current traffic, time of day, and passenger load. Grace gets back:"

*Show the reply SMS on screen:*
```
[Ma3] 46 CBD-Westlands inakuja Westlands 
in ~4 min. Seats: 6. Fare: KSh 50. 
Lipa: *384*1#
```

> "No app. No data. Just SMS. That's inclusive mobility."

---

### LIVE DEMO — ACT 2: THE DRIVER (30 seconds)
*Dial `*384#` on second phone.*

> "James is our driver. He dials `*384#` — works on any Safaricom phone."

*Navigate through USSD menu live:*
- `1` → Check-in kwa route
- `1` → 46 CBD-Westlands
- `10` → passenger count

> "James just checked in. That ping hits our FastAPI backend, updates his position, and broadcasts to the dashboard via WebSocket."

*Point at map on laptop — dot moves.*

> "The SACCO manager sees it in real time."

---

### LIVE DEMO — ACT 3: THE SACCO DASHBOARD (40 seconds)
*Turn laptop to face judges.*

> "This is what Mr. Kamau — the SACCO manager — sees right now."

Point to each section:

> **Map:** "Three vehicles, moving on actual Nairobi streets. CBD to Westlands in real time."

> **Demand Forecast:** "Our XGBoost model is predicting passenger load per route for the next hour. Route 34 is about to hit HIGH demand — the system flags it. Mr. Kamau redeploys a vehicle before the peak, not after."

> **Driver Leaderboard:** "Every driver has a safety score — 0 to 100 — generated by an Isolation Forest anomaly detection model. Speed variance, off-route behaviour, harsh braking. James is at 87. Safe. If a driver drops below 50, the SACCO gets an alert."

> **Live Telemetry:** "Every ping, every stop, every passenger count — streaming live."

---

### THE ML STACK (20 seconds)
> "Three models, each solving a real problem:"

| Model | Algorithm | Metric |
|---|---|---|
| ETA prediction | LSTM | MAE: 0.65 min |
| Demand forecast | XGBoost | R²: 0.9564 |
| Driver scoring | Isolation Forest | ROC-AUC: 1.0 |

> "Trained on Nairobi route patterns, peak hours, SACCO data. Running live on this machine right now."

### AFRICA'S TALKING INTEGRATION (15 seconds)
> "We use three Africa's Talking APIs:"
- **SMS** — commuter ETA alerts, fare receipts, driver summaries
- **USSD** — driver check-ins and wallet access, zero data required
- **Payments** — M-Pesa STK push for cashless fares, driver payouts

> "Every API call you saw in this demo was real — hitting the Africa's Talking sandbox."

### SCALE STORY (15 seconds)
